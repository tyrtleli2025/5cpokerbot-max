general guide for preflop probability
this is considering we toss a RANDOM card and our opponent does as well

TRIPS:
probability ranges around .9

PAIRS:
    Suited:
        .5 < p < .8
        p > .7 when all cards are above ten
    Unsuited:
        very similar range, just slightly lower
        can treat them the same pretty much

HIGH CARD:
    3-Suited:
        .5 < p < .7
    2-Suited:
        .4 < p <.6
        roughly, like 10% of these fall below .4, and even less go above .6
    Rainbow:
        .35 < p < .5
        goes above .5 if multiple high cards
